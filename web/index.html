    How Noctis works

    1. Scan. The Bitget Agent CLI reads every tokenized US stock pair and measures the move since the last US close.
    2. Research. Bitget Signal tools fetch news, rates, Fed headlines, sentiment and the real stock prices.
    3. Write. An AI model turns only that evidence into your briefing. No evidence, no explanation.
    4. Deliver. A scheduled job runs every night, so the page is fresh when you wake up.

    Noctis provides analysis, not financial advice. You make the final call. Built for the Bitget AI Hackathon S2, Track 3: Personalized research workstation.
